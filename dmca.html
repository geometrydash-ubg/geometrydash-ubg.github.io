DMCA and Copyright

Report a copyright concern about material on Geometry Dash Unblocked by identifying the work, the exact page, and your authority to act. Use the relevant notice details rather than a general gameplay complaint.

📝 Send a specific copyright notice

Send rights requests to contact@geometrydash-ubg.com with the subject “Copyright notice.” Identify the protected work, the material at issue, and its exact location on this site. Geometry Dash names, game art, music, and other protected materials remain the property of their respective owners.

  • A physical or electronic signature from the owner or authorized representative.
  • Identification of the work, or a representative list for multiple works.
  • Enough detail to locate the disputed material, including the page address.
  • Your name and contact details, including an address, telephone number, and email where available.
  • A good-faith statement that the disputed use lacks permission from the owner, agent, or law.
  • A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you may act for the rights owner.

🔍 Review and response

A notice needs enough information to locate the material and assess the rights claim. Relevant notice details may need to be shared with a person whose material is affected; do not include unrelated private information. Review focuses on the identified material on this site.

Gameplay questions, loading failures, and corrections to a guide belong in an ordinary site report. A copyright notice should identify a rights concern rather than a general complaint.

↩️ A claim of mistake or misidentification

For an ordinary review of a mistaken removal, identify the material, its former page, and why you believe it was misidentified. State whether you are asking for a correction or submitting a formal statutory counter-notice; those are different requests.

A formal DMCA counter-notice requires a physical or electronic signature, identification of the removed material and its former location, and a good-faith statement under penalty of perjury that removal resulted from mistake or misidentification. It also requires your name, address, telephone number, consent to the relevant United States federal court’s jurisdiction, and agreement to accept service from the claimant or their agent. For a United States address, the relevant district covers that address; outside the United States, it is a district where the service provider may be found.

A request for review does not by itself promise restoration on a fixed date. Where the statutory DMCA counter-notice procedure applies, it involves forwarding a valid counter-notice and a 10–14-business-day period, subject to notice of a qualifying court action. A general correction request is not automatically that statutory process.
